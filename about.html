Our Story

Built on values. Ready for the future.

Meteitei Boys Senior School stands as a beacon of character, scholarship and leadership in Tinderet. Since its founding in 1975, the school has grown into a disciplined learning community dedicated to academic excellence and holistic development.

Our Mission

To nurture every learner into a responsible, God-fearing and capable young man who contributes positively to family, community and nation. We equip students with knowledge, values and confidence to thrive in a rapidly changing world.

Our school culture is grounded in discipline, diligence, respect and service. Through academic structure, moral instruction and active co-curricular engagement, every boy is challenged to become a well-rounded citizen.

What We Champion

  • Academic excellence and disciplined study habits
  • Integrity, responsibility and community service
  • Leadership development and student voice
  • Innovation, creativity and holistic growth
