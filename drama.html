Creative Arts

Drama Club

Drama gives students a space to explore creativity, confidence and empathy through storytelling, performance and teamwork.

Members of the drama club take part in acting, script development, stage performance and expressive communication. These experiences help learners build confidence and emotional intelligence.

  • Performance and acting skills
  • Storytelling and confidence building
  • Teamwork and cultural expression
