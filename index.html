Who We Are

A School Built on Values & Vision

Meteitei School is committed to academic excellence and the holistic development of every learner. We provide a nurturing environment where students grow intellectually, socially, and morally.

Our mission is to prepare learners for a dynamic, competitive world — instilling curiosity, discipline, and compassion in everything we do.

Our Story

Rooted in Purpose,
Growing Through Time

Founded to provide quality education in Nandi County, Meteitei School has grown from humble beginnings to become a cornerstone institution in the region. Every decade has brought new milestones — and an unwavering commitment to the children we serve.

Est.
School founded to serve the Nandi County community with quality primary education.
ICT
Modern computer labs introduced, bringing digital skills to every learner.
Sec.
Secondary section launched, offering full progression within one campus.
Now
Serving 500+ students, with consistently strong national examination results.
