Principal's Message

A Word from Our Principal

"Jifunze uelimike — we believe in discipline, hard work, integrity and excellence, producing talented, holistic boys who will be men of distinction."

Welcome to Meteitei Boys Secondary School. It is my privilege to lead this institution, founded in 1975 on land donated by the Taptengelei community and built up by generations of board chairpersons, parents, and well-wishers.

Our dedicated staff, supportive community, and hardworking students continue to make us proud every day. We believe in the full potential of every boy who walks through our gates, and we commit to providing the environment, resources, and guidance they need to thrive.

Mr. Barnabas Too Chief Principal, since 2017 Meteitei Boys Secondary School

Who We Are

A School Built on Values & Vision

Our vision is to be the epitome of success. Our mission is to create an enabling environment that nurtures talents and educates individuals to be reliable, holistic and flexible to changes in the modern world.

From a single stream at founding, the school has grown to four streams with 1,800+ students, 45 TSC teachers, 40 BOM teachers and 47 support staff.

Our Story

Rooted in Purpose, Growing Through Time

On 19th May 1975, the foundation stone was laid by His Excellency Daniel Toroitich Arap Moi, then Vice President, flagged off by MP Jean Marie Seroney. The land was a gift from the Taptengelei community — and almost everything since has been added by hand, harvest, and constituency fund.

1975
Foundation stone laid by H.E. Daniel Toroitich Arap Moi, then Vice President. Land donated by the Taptengelei community.
1980
Hon. Henry Kosgey helps build Kosgey, Barng'etuny and Abura dormitories as BOM chairperson.
1988
Hon. Joseph K. Letting supports eight teachers' houses, Tendenei dormitory, power installation, and the dining hall.
2004
Mr. Paul K. Lilan's 18-year tenure brings form four blocks, Kiptarus & Ng'orbobit dormitories, CCTV, and a standby generator.
Now
Under Dr. Thomas Bor, serving 1,039 students with Phase IV construction ongoing through CDF and T.I.G.
